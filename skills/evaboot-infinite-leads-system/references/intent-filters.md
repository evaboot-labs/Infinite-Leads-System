# Intent filters and copy

One intent filter per saved search, so each campaign speaks to one reason for reaching out.
Replace the `{{placeholders}}` with your own words before the campaign goes live.

## Lead signals

Sales Navigator lead search, groups **Buyer intent** and **Recent updates**. Ask `build_sn_search_from_prompt` for the ICP plus the signal in plain words.

### Following your company

- **What it means:** They follow your company page on LinkedIn.
- **Why it works:** They already know you exist, so your email is not a cold one.
- **Opener:** You follow {{companyName}} on LinkedIn, so you have probably seen what we do with {{yourTopic}}.
- **Follow-up:** Since you follow our updates, I thought the {{caseStudy}} result would be relevant: {{oneLineResult}}.

### Viewed your profile recently

- **What it means:** They looked at your own LinkedIn profile recently.
- **Why it works:** They were curious about you first. Answering that curiosity feels natural.
- **Opener:** Saw you checked my profile, so I'll keep this short.
- **Follow-up:** Was it {{yourTopic}} that caught your eye? Happy to share how {{similarCompany}} handles it.

### Changed jobs

- **What it means:** They started a new role in the last 90 days.
- **Why it works:** New leaders review their tools and budgets in their first 90 days.
- **Opener:** Congrats on the new role at {{companyName}}. The first 90 days are when most {{jobTitle}}s rethink {{yourTopic}}.
- **Follow-up:** When {{similarPerson}} joined {{similarCompany}}, the first thing they fixed was {{problem}}. Worth a look?

### Posted on LinkedIn

- **What it means:** They published a post in the last 30 days.
- **Why it works:** Active posters read their notifications and answer people who engage with their ideas.
- **Opener:** Your recent post on {{postTopic}} made me think of a problem we solve for {{jobTitle}}s.
- **Follow-up:** Following up on {{postTopic}}: here is the one-page version of how {{similarCompany}} approached it.

## Account signals

Sales Navigator account search (**Spotlights**, **Company attributes**). These go through an account list: see the skill, section "Extra: company signals through an account list".

### Hiring on LinkedIn

- **What it means:** The company has open roles on LinkedIn (Job opportunities filter).
- **Why it works:** Open roles show where the company is investing, and growth brings new needs and new budget.
- **Opener:** Saw {{companyName}} is hiring for {{role}}, which usually means {{yourTopic}} is about to get busier.
- **Follow-up:** Teams that hire {{role}}s often hit {{problem}} first. Here is how {{similarCompany}} avoided it.

### Senior leadership changes in last 3 months

- **What it means:** A new senior leader joined in the last three months (Recent activities filter).
- **Why it works:** New leaders bring new priorities and often new vendors.
- **Opener:** Noticed {{companyName}} brought in a new {{leaderTitle}} recently. Leadership changes are usually when {{yourTopic}} gets a fresh look.
- **Follow-up:** If {{yourTopic}} is part of the new plan, I can show you what worked for {{similarCompany}} in a 15-minute call.

### Funding events in past 12 months

- **What it means:** The company raised money in the last year (Recent activities filter).
- **Why it works:** Fresh funding comes with growth targets and budget to hit them.
- **Opener:** Congrats on the {{fundingRound}}. Most teams we work with use that moment to scale {{yourTopic}}.
- **Follow-up:** Post-raise growth plans usually stall on {{problem}}. Here is how {{similarCompany}} solved it in a month.

### Company headcount growth

- **What it means:** The company's headcount grew within the Min (%) to Max (%) range you set (Company attributes group).
- **Why it works:** A team that is growing fast outgrows its current processes and tools.
- **Opener:** {{companyName}} has grown fast lately. Congrats to the team: growth like that usually puts pressure on {{yourTopic}}.
- **Follow-up:** When {{similarCompany}} grew at the same pace, {{problem}} was the first thing to break. Here is how they fixed it.
